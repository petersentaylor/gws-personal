Privacy policy

gws-personal is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data.

  • Data is read and changed only at the owner's request, on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
